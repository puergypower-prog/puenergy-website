About PUERGY

A practical partner for your solar journey.

PUERGY is being built as a solar products, distribution and EPC solutions company focused on making dependable solar technology more accessible.

We bring together solar module sourcing, channel distribution, rooftop installations and commercial and industrial project solutions. Our approach is built around quality-conscious sourcing, practical project design, transparent communication and responsive customer support.

  • Solar modules sourced from established manufacturers, subject to availability and supply arrangements.
  • Solutions for residential rooftops, commercial buildings, factories and agricultural applications.
  • Project delivery through suitable technical, installation and service partners.
  • Progressive expansion into inverters, storage and hybrid energy solutions.
